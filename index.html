Flagomania

Welcome to Flagomania – a fun and educational game for the whole family! Test your knowledge of Europe flags by guessing the country each flag represents. Click the button to reveal a random flag and try to guess it before checking the answer. It's a great way to learn about different countries, cultures, and geography while having fun. Play together, challenge each other, and turn learning into an exciting game!

Guess the flag!

Click the button to see a random flag from somewhere in Europe. Try to guess the country in your head — if you're not sure, click "Reveal Country" to check the answer.
